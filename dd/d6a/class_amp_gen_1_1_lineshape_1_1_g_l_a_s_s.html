Lineshapes

Detailed Description

Alternative parameterisation of the \( K \pi \) S-wave taken from the BABAR/BELLE analysis of \( D^{0} \to K_{S}^{0} \pi^{+} \pi^{-} \) https://journals.aps.org/prd/pdf/10.1103/PhysRevD.98.112012. It introduces four additional parameters, two amplitudes and two phases, and is written

\[ \mathcal{A}(s) = R\sin\delta_R e^{i(\delta_R + 2\delta_F)} + F\sin\delta_F e^{i\delta_F} \]

where

\[ \delta_R = \phi_R + \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{m\Gamma(s)}{m^2 -s }\right) \]

and

\[ \delta_F = \phi_F + \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{2 a q}{2 + arq^2}\right), \]

that is, the 'resonant' and 'nonresonant' phases of the LASS formulation with additional 'proudction' phases \( \phi_R \) and \(\phi_F \), and production couplings \(R, F\). One of the production couplings can typically be fixed to 1, normally the resonant coupling, \(R \).
